## Reflection

The claim that looked most authoritative but was actually weakest was claim #3 regarding IIM Bangalore’s ₹35.31 LPA average placement package. Gemini presented the statistic confidently with a precise number and institution name, which made it appear trustworthy. Perplexity-style secondary references initially seemed supportive, but when I searched official IIM Bangalore placement sources, I could not find any primary report confirming the exact figure. This showed that highly specific AI-generated statistics can still lack verifiable evidence. The exercise reinforced that confidence and specificity do not guarantee factual correctness, and that human verification using primary sources is essential.
